WHY THIS STUDIO

它不是另一个“AI 帮我写个视频稿”。This is not another “AI, write me a video script.”

真正困难的不是生成句子，而是决定什么值得拍、为什么由你来讲、观众会得到什么，以及在投入拍摄前怎样发现问题。工作台把这些决定放回脚本之前。The hard part is deciding what deserves a video, why you should tell it, what viewers gain, and how to catch problems before production. This studio moves those decisions upstream.

01 CREATOR FIT

先看你能持续做什么

把经验、资源、镜头舒适度、时间预算和隐私边界变成创作约束。Turn experience, access, camera comfort, time, budget, and privacy into creative constraints.

02 EVIDENCE

事实和创意分层

区分已验证事实、创作者提供的信息与创作选择，不编数据和经历。Separate verified facts, creator-supplied claims, and creative choices. Never invent metrics or experience.

03 DECISION

选题不是打分游戏

最终给出推进、缩小、换角度、搁置或放弃的明确建议。End with a decision: pursue, narrow, re-angle, park, or reject.

04 FORMAT

形式服务于材料

知识口播、Vlog 和喜剧独白按内容引擎与制作风险比较。Compare talking-head, Vlog, and comedy by narrative engine and production risk.

05 PREVIEW

拍摄前先看见成片

用时间线、前 30–45 秒、视听表和删改清单做低成本预演。Preview with a timeline, full opening, audiovisual table, and prioritized cut list.

06 LEARNING

复盘只改最值得改的

用真实表现和制作成本，选择下一期一到三个可验证实验。Use real performance and production cost to choose one to three testable changes.

THREE ENGINES

同一个主题，可以有三种完全不同的生命。One topic can live through three very different engines.

工作台不会把“视频类型”当作模板标签，而会判断你的材料靠什么向前推进。Formats are not template labels. The studio asks what actually moves the material forward.

FORMAT 01

知识口播
Knowledge talking-head

问题 → 解释 → 证据 → 使用。适合研究、经验、演示和复杂概念。Question → explanation → proof → use. Best for research, expertise, demos, and complex ideas.

主要风险：准确，却像一堂没有观众任务的课。
FORMAT 02

Vlog
Process-led story

目标 → 尝试 → 变化 → 反思。适合真实过程、地点、挑战与人物关系。Goal → attempts → change → reflection. Best for process, place, challenge, and relationships.

主要风险：拍了很多，但没有故事发生。
FORMAT 03

喜剧独白
Comedic monologue

前提 → 升级 → 包袱 → 回扣。适合强烈个人观点和生活矛盾。Premise → escalation → punch → callback. Best for personal point of view and contradiction.

主要风险：有笑点，却没有完整论点。

预览交付标准Preview delivery standard

  • 一段话说明观众会经历什么。A one-paragraph viewer experience.
  • 时间线或音画表，标明每段任务。A timeline or A/V table with a job for every beat.
  • 完整写出前 30–45 秒。The first 30–45 seconds written in full.
  • 时长估算及语速假设。Runtime estimate and speaking-rate assumption.
  • 承诺兑现、证据与合规风险检查。Promise, evidence, and compliance checks.
  • 按优先级排列的删改清单。A prioritized cut and change list.

FAQ

先把最重要的边界说清楚。The important boundaries, made explicit.

会保证播放量或涨粉吗？Does it guarantee views or growth?

不会。它提高选题、证据、结构和复盘质量；真实结果只能通过发布验证。No. It improves decisions, evidence, structure, and learning. Results require real publication.

能直接写完整逐字稿吗？Can it draft a full script directly?

可以。若形态或语气选择会显著改变结果，工作台会先给最小样稿或说明采用的默认方案。Yes. When format or tone materially changes the result, it previews or states the chosen default first.

会模仿某个在世创作者吗？Will it imitate a living creator?

不会直接仿写。它会把参考转成节奏、密度、幽默度等抽象特征。No direct imitation. References become abstract traits such as pace, density, or humor.

可以只做审稿吗？Can it audit without rewriting?

可以。审阅模式默认只诊断标题、结构、证据和制作风险，除非明确要求才重写。Yes. Audit mode diagnoses packaging, structure, evidence, and production risk unless rewriting is requested.
